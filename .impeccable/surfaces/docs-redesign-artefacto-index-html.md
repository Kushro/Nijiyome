# Surface brief: Nijiyome redesign explorer (artifact)

Scope: an interactive HTML artifact that presents the full Nijiyome redesign as alternatives to mix (directions, structures per key screen, card styles, motion profiles, themes), for desktop and Android, plus a parity matrix proving no functionality is lost. Mode: operate (the screens shown are app UI); the explorer chrome around them is quiet.

Audience: the owner (power user, designer-developer) choosing a final direction. Job: compare, mix, decide. Constraint: every proposal maps to the parity inventory; Material 3 structure on Android; light, dark, OLED.

The decision page is the artifact itself: the owner asked to see many clearly different alternatives side by side and mix them, so directions are presented in the artifact instead of a one-choice decision round.

## Direction contract

THESIS: What you want to watch hangs like wishes from bamboo; the rest of the app is a clean night tool. It refuses the streaming-clone default: a grey-black ground with identical poster rows and one purple accent.

OWN-WORLD: Night indigo ground, washi paper strips in the five tanzaku colors used as real status colors (watching, planning, completed, paused, dropped), lantern amber reserved for focus and "now", bamboo green hairlines as rails. A rounded Japanese-friendly sans for UI, a brush-handwriting voice only for short wish lines.

STORY: The owner sees tonight's wishes (continue and next episodes), picks one, sees episodes and sources with honest states, plays with enhancements and Link visible, and reaches any setting in two steps or through Ctrl+K.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Shell first: left rail (6 primary destinations, 4 utility), top bar with command search. Home: a bamboo rail of hanging tanzaku for "Esta noche" across the top third; below, today's airing timeline; right column with Link and transfers status.

FORM: grounded candidate 5 of 7 (Tanabata night), seed:97573c6a69594286.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Alternates presented alongside

- Obi (impeccable's pick): light novel and Blu-ray obi bands as the card and status language; spines for lists.
- Midnight transit (challenger, competitive): series as lines, episodes as stations, Link as an interchange.
- Sumi and hanko (identity-keeping track): current violet, ensō kitsune, seals as status badges.
- Canon: Material 3 played straight.
- Departure board (challenger, competitive on clarity): airing calendar as a departures board, offered as a calendar structure in every direction.

Raises kept from declined challengers: destructive actions isolated (dark developer console); one control with a live preview (variable font specimen); layouts reflow by whole card cells (Crouwel grid); state encoded in form, decoded on hover or long-press (Saville catalog sleeve).
